Use case: ui-mockup / stylized-concept.
Create one polished illustrative concept image for an original turn-based dark fantasy 2D dungeon game named Cripta. This is a visual design proposal, not a screenshot of an implemented game. Landscape composition, top-down overhead room with painterly illustrated game sprites, NOT pixel art, NOT 3D render, no isometric perspective.
The large central playfield is a readable square crypt chamber seen from above: worn stone floor, restrained bone and candle decoration around the perimeter, doors on cardinal walls. A clearly recognizable cloaked human adventurer with a blade stands toward the lower-left center. Three clearly separated original fantasy enemies stand elsewhere within the SAME room: an armored skeletal guardian with shield, a ragged spectral wanderer, and a lean beastlike hunter. Each is a distinct well-defined game sprite with clear silhouettes and consistent overhead camera. A small potion bottle and a rolled parchment lie on the floor, distinct from decor.
A selected guardian has a subtle amber selection ring and small life bar; a restrained curved slash effect communicates a single confirmed turn-based attack. The room remains the exploration scene during combat, no separate battle scene. Keep spaces spacious and legible, moody cool desaturated stone with warm candlelight, tasteful hand-painted dark fantasy illustration, no gore.
UI is secondary: a slim upper player portrait/health strip, a small fog-of-war room minimap in the top-right with just visited and adjacent room cells, and a compact bottom command strip with the exact Spanish labels "Atacar", "Objetos", "Esperar", "Mapa". Do not put enemy monsters in the minimap beyond abstract remembered marks. The detailed room occupies most of the image. Original characters and artwork, not copies of any existing game. No logos, no watermark. Focus on the visual promise: an illustrated room inhabited by the player, enemies and objects, rather than a dashboard or abstract graph.
